Offline PDF Editor - Licence and Privacy Notice
================================================

PRIVACY
-------
Offline PDF Editor processes every document entirely on this computer.

  * Documents are never uploaded, synchronised or sent to any server.
  * OCR, conversion, compression, signing and redaction run locally.
  * There is no telemetry, analytics, crash reporting or advertising.
  * No account or sign-in is required. No internet connection is required.
  * Update checks are not performed.

Temporary working copies, autosave/recovery data and backups are stored in
your local user profile (%APPDATA%\Offline PDF Editor) and are cleaned up
according to the retention settings in Preferences > Privacy.

Your original files are never overwritten unless you explicitly choose
Save; "Save As" always writes a new file.
